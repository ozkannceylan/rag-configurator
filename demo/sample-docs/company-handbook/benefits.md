# Employee Benefits Guide

## Overview

TechCorp offers a comprehensive benefits package designed to support your health, financial security, work-life balance, and professional growth. This guide provides detailed information about all available benefits and how to access them.

**Eligibility**: Most benefits are available to full-time employees (30+ hours/week) from day one. Part-time employees (20-29 hours) are eligible for select benefits after 90 days.

**Enrollment**: New hires must enroll within 30 days of start date. Annual open enrollment occurs in November for January 1 effective date. Life events (marriage, birth, etc.) allow mid-year changes within 30 days.

## 401(k) Retirement Plan

### Plan Overview

**Eligibility**: Immediate enrollment available; company match begins after 1 year of service

**Contribution Limits** (2025):
- Employee contribution: Up to $23,500 annually
- Age 50+ catch-up: Additional $7,500
- After-tax contributions: Available up to $46,000 total

### Company Match

**Matching Formula:**
- 100% match on first 3% of salary contributed
- 50% match on next 2% of salary contributed
- **Maximum match**: 4% of salary when contributing 5%

**Example**: Employee earning $100,000 contributes 5% ($5,000)
- Company contributes: $4,000 (100% of first $3,000 + 50% of next $2,000)
- Total annual contribution: $9,000

**Vesting Schedule**:
- 0-2 years: 0% vested (forfeit match if leaving)
- 2 years: 40% vested
- 3 years: 60% vested
- 4 years: 80% vested
- 5+ years: 100% vested

### Investment Options

**Core Menu** (18 funds):
- Target-date funds (Lifecycle 2025-2065)
- Large, mid, small-cap index funds
- International equity funds
- Bond funds (short, intermediate, long-term)
- Stable value fund
- Company stock (optional, max 20% of portfolio)

**Self-Directed Brokerage**: Available for experienced investors (Fidelity)

**Default Investment**: Lifecycle target-date fund based on age 65 retirement

### Plan Features

**Loans**: Borrow up to 50% of vested balance ($50,000 max)
- Repayment: 5 years (15 years for primary residence)
- Interest: Prime + 1%, paid to yourself
- No credit check required

**Hardship Withdrawals**: Available for:
- Medical expenses
- Home purchase (primary residence)
- Tuition and education fees
- Prevent eviction/foreclosure
- Funeral expenses

**Roth 401(k) Option**: Post-tax contributions with tax-free growth

## Additional Financial Benefits

### Life and Disability Insurance

**Basic Life Insurance** (Company Paid):
- 1x annual salary, rounded to nearest $10,000
- Minimum: $50,000
- Maximum: $500,000
- Spouse coverage: $10,000
- Child coverage: $5,000 per child

**Supplemental Life Insurance**:
- Employee: Up to 5x salary (guaranteed issue up to 2x)
- Spouse: $10,000 - $100,000
- Children: $5,000 - $25,000
- Rates based on age and smoking status

**Disability Insurance:**

**Short-Term Disability (STD)**:
- Duration: Up to 26 weeks
- Benefit: 60% of weekly salary, max $2,500/week
- Elimination period: 7 days (accident) / 14 days (illness)
- Company paid

**Long-Term Disability (LTD)**:
- Duration: To age 65 or Social Security retirement
- Benefit: 60% of monthly salary, max $15,000/month
- Elimination period: 180 days
- Company paid
- Coordination with SSDI

### Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

**Healthcare FSA**:
- Annual limit: $3,300 (2025)
- $610 rollover permitted (or grace period)
- Eligible expenses: Deductibles, copays, prescriptions, dental, vision, OTC items
- Debit card provided for convenience

**Dependent Care FSA**:
- Annual limit: $5,000 ($2,500 if married filing separately)
- Eligible expenses: Childcare, elder care, before/after school programs
- Summer camps eligible
- Must be for care enabling you to work

**Commuter Benefits**:
- Transit: Up to $315/month pre-tax
- Parking: Up to $315/month pre-tax
- Combined limit: $630/month

### Financial Planning and Education

**Resources Available:**
- Free 1-on-1 financial advisor consultations (Fidelity)
- Online planning tools and calculators
- Annual financial wellness workshops
- Student loan repayment guidance
- First-time homebuyer seminars

## How to Enroll and Manage Benefits

### Enrollment Process
1. Log into Workday within 30 days of hire
2. Navigate to Benefits → Enroll
3. Select desired coverage for each benefit category
4. Add dependents (requires documentation)
5. Designate beneficiaries for insurance
6. Review and confirm selections
7. Print confirmation for records

### Making Changes

**Qualifying Life Events** (30-day window):
- Marriage, divorce, legal separation
- Birth, adoption, or placement for adoption
- Death of spouse or dependent
- Change in employment status (spouse)
- Loss of other coverage
- Change in residence affecting plan availability
- Eligibility for Medicare/Medicaid

**Documentation Required:**
- Marriage: Marriage certificate
- Birth: Birth certificate
- Adoption: Court order
- Loss of coverage: Letter from previous insurer
