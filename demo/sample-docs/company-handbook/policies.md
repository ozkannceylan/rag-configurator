## Remote Work Policy

### Overview
At TechCorp, we embrace flexible work arrangements that support both employee well-being and business objectives. This policy outlines guidelines for remote work eligibility, expectations, and best practices.

### Eligibility
- All full-time employees with 6+ months tenure are eligible to request remote work
- Remote work requests must be approved by direct manager
- Certain roles requiring physical presence (facilities, on-site support) may have limited remote options
- Part-time remote work (2-3 days/week) is the default; full-time remote requires VP approval

### Work Environment Requirements
- **Home Office**: Employees must maintain a dedicated, quiet workspace
- **Internet**: Minimum 25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload required
- **Equipment**: Company provides laptop, monitor, and peripherals; employee responsible for ergonomic furniture
- **Security**: Home network must use WPA2/WPA3 encryption; VPN required for all work activities

### Availability and Communication
- **Core Hours**: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM in your local timezone for meetings and collaboration
- **Response Time**: Slack messages within 2 hours during business hours; emails within 24 hours
- **Status Updates**: Post daily standup updates in team channel by 9:30 AM
- **Meetings**: Camera-on policy for all video calls to maintain connection

### Performance Expectations
Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as in-office staff:
- Project deliverables and deadlines remain unchanged
- Participation in team activities and company events is expected
- Annual performance reviews use identical criteria
- Excessive remote work that impacts collaboration may result in modified arrangements

### Expense Reimbursement
- Internet stipend: $50/month reimbursement with receipt
- Coworking space: Up to $200/month with manager approval
- Home office setup: One-time $500 allowance for ergonomic equipment
- Travel to office: Covered if required more than twice per month

## Vacation and Time Off

### Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Annual PTO Allowance by Tenure:**
- 0-2 years: 15 days (3 weeks)
- 3-5 years: 20 days (4 weeks)
- 6+ years: 25 days (5 weeks)
- Executive team: 30 days (6 weeks)

**Additional Leave Types:**
- **Sick Leave**: 10 days per year, rolling over up to 30 days
- **Personal Days**: 3 days per year, use for mental health, appointments, or personal matters
- **Bereavement**: Up to 5 days paid leave for immediate family, 3 days for extended family
- **Jury Duty**: Full pay for duration of service
- **Parental Leave**: See detailed policy below
- **Volunteer Time**: 2 paid days per year for approved nonprofit work

### Requesting Time Off
1. Submit request in Workday at least 2 weeks in advance (1 month for requests >5 days)
2. Manager approval required; denials must include business justification
3. Blackout periods apply for certain roles during peak seasons (December for retail, tax season for accounting)
4. Maximum consecutive time off without VP approval: 15 business days

### Parental Leave Policy
- **Birth Parent**: 16 weeks fully paid leave
- **Non-Birth Parent**: 8 weeks fully paid leave
- **Adoption/Foster**: 8 weeks paid leave from placement date
- **Return-to-work**: Flexible return options available (part-time transition, remote work)
- **Benefits**: Health insurance continues during leave; 401k matching paused

### Unlimited PTO (Leadership Track)
- Director-level and above eligible for unlimited PTO
- Minimum usage required: 15 days annually to prevent burnout
- Same request and approval process applies
- Abuse policy: Pattern of minimal usage may trigger HR review

### Holiday Schedule
**Company-Wide Holidays (11 days):**
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (January, third Monday)
- Presidents' Day (February, third Monday)
- Memorial Day (May, last Monday)
- Juneteenth (June 19)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (September, first Monday)
- Thanksgiving Day + Day After
- Christmas Eve (December 24, half day)
- Christmas Day (December 25)
- Floating Holiday (employee choice)

## Code of Conduct

### Our Values
1. **Integrity**: We act with honesty and strong moral principles
2. **Respect**: We treat everyone with dignity and value diverse perspectives
3. **Excellence**: We strive for exceptional quality in everything we do
4. **Collaboration**: We work together to achieve common goals
5. **Innovation**: We embrace creativity and continuous improvement

### Professional Conduct
**Communication Standards:**
- Use respectful, professional language in all interactions
- No harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind
- Constructive feedback is encouraged; personal attacks are prohibited
- Confidential information must remain confidential
- Social media posts should not disparage the company, colleagues, or clients

**Workplace Behavior:**
- Arrive on time for meetings and commitments
- Complete assigned tasks by deadlines
- Support colleagues and foster a positive team environment
- Report violations of this code without fear of retaliation
- Maintain appropriate boundaries with colleagues and clients

**Conflict of Interest:**
- Disclose any relationships that might create conflicts
- Avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company interests
- Do not accept gifts over $100 from vendors or clients
- Outside employment must be disclosed and cannot compete with company business

### Reporting Violations
Violations should be reported to:
1. Direct manager (for minor concerns)
2. HR Business Partner
3. Anonymous Ethics Hotline: 1-800-XXX-XXXX
4. Legal/Compliance team for serious matters

**Anti-Retaliation**: Retaliation against anyone who reports violations in good faith is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate termination.

### Consequences
Violations will be addressed based on severity:
- **Minor violations**: Verbal warning, coaching
- **Moderate violations**: Written warning, performance improvement plan
- **Serious violations**: Suspension, termination, potential legal action
- **Illegal conduct**: Immediate termination, law enforcement notification

## Confidentiality Agreement

### Definition of Confidential Information
Confidential information includes but is not limited to:
- Trade secrets and proprietary technology
- Customer lists and business relationships
- Financial data and strategic plans
- Employee personal information
- Product development roadmaps
- Unpublished research and intellectual property
- Security protocols and infrastructure details
- M&A activities and negotiations

### Employee Obligations
- Maintain strict confidentiality of all proprietary information
- Do not disclose confidential information to unauthorized parties
- Protect company data from unauthorized access
- Return all company materials upon termination
- Continue confidentiality obligations after employment ends
- Report any suspected breaches immediately

### Permitted Disclosures
Confidential information may be disclosed when:
- Required by law or court order (notify Legal immediately)
- Necessary to perform job duties for authorized personnel
- Information becomes publicly available through no fault of employee
- Approved in writing by CEO or General Counsel

### Data Protection Requirements
- Use company-approved tools for file sharing
- Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest
- Lock computers when away from desk
- Do not store company data on personal devices
- Follow password policies and MFA requirements
- Complete annual security training

## Technology Usage

### Acceptable Use Policy

**Company Equipment:**
- Laptops, phones, and devices are company property
- Personal use is permitted for incidental, reasonable use
- Do not install unauthorized software or apps
- Keep systems updated with security patches
- Report lost or stolen equipment within 24 hours

**Internet and Network:**
- Business use takes priority over personal use
- No illegal downloads or pirated content
- Streaming services allowed during breaks only
- Do not bypass network security measures
- Guest network available for personal devices

**Email Communication:**
- Company email is for business purposes
- Personal emails should be minimal and appropriate
- No chain letters, spam, or mass non-business emails
- Attachments over 25MB should use company file sharing
- Retention policy: 7 years for business emails

**Software and Licenses:**
- Use only licensed, approved software
- No torrenting or cracked applications
- Open source usage must comply with license terms
- IT must approve all software installations
- Cloud services require security review

### Prohibited Activities
The following are strictly prohibited:
- Accessing or distributing pornographic content
- Harassment or discriminatory communications
- Hacking attempts on company systems
- Installing malware or malicious software
- Using company resources for personal business
- Cryptocurrency mining on company hardware
- Sharing login credentials
- Circumventing data loss prevention (DLP) tools

### Monitoring Notice
Company reserves the right to monitor:
- Email and Slack communications
- Internet browsing history
- File downloads and uploads
- Application usage
- Device location (company phones)

Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems.

## Anti-Discrimination Policy

### Policy Statement
TechCorp is committed to providing equal employment opportunities and creating an inclusive workplace free from discrimination and harassment.

### Protected Characteristics
We do not discriminate based on:
- Race, color, ethnicity, or national origin
- Religion, creed, or spiritual beliefs
- Gender, gender identity, or gender expression
- Sexual orientation
- Age (40 and older)
- Disability (physical or mental)
- Veteran or military status
- Genetic information
- Marital or familial status
- Pregnancy or related medical conditions

### Harassment Prohibition
Harassment includes:
- Offensive jokes, slurs, or derogatory comments
- Unwelcome sexual advances or propositions
- Display of offensive images or objects
- Intimidation, threats, or hostile behavior
- Interference with work performance
- Retaliation for reporting discrimination

### Accommodation
- **Religious**: Reasonable accommodations for religious practices and observances
- **Disability**: ADA-compliant accommodations for qualified individuals with disabilities
- **Pregnancy**: Accommodations for pregnancy-related limitations
- **Language**: Translation services available for non-English speakers

### Complaint Procedure
1. Report to HR immediately
2. Investigation will be conducted within 10 business days
3. Interim measures may be implemented during investigation
4. Findings communicated to parties involved
5. Corrective action taken if violation confirmed
6. Appeal process available through Legal

### Training Requirements
- Annual anti-discrimination and harassment training required for all employees
- Manager-specific training on handling complaints
- Bystander intervention training
- Unconscious bias training for hiring managers
